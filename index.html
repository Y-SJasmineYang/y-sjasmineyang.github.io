About Me

I am a Research Associate (Postdoctoral Researcher) at the University of Galway and a PhD Candidate in Economics at University College Dublin. My PhD viva is scheduled for January 2027, and I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.


My research is primarily in behavioural economics, using experimental and theoretical approaches to study how people make decisions under different environments. It follows two broad streams: individual and collective decisions.


In the first stream, my research examines motivation and investment. My PhD work studies effort in all-pay contests, investment under algorithmic discrimination in hiring, and whether beliefs about payoff variance help explain ambiguity aversion. A separate project investigates how information about relative income rank affects charitable giving.


My second stream examines how people’s decisions depend on others. My postdoctoral work at the University of Galway develops this interest in collective decision-making.

Research Interests: Behavioral Economics, Experimental Economics, Game Theory, Environmental Economics, Algorithmic Fairness
