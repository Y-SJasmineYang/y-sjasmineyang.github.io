Hi, I’m Yung-Shiang Jasmine Yang. I’m a Research Associate (Postdoctoral Researcher) at the University of Galway and a PhD Candidate in Economics at University College Dublin. My viva is scheduled for January 2027, and I’m on the 2026–2027 academic job market.


My research is in behavioural economics. I use experiments and theoretical models to study how people make decisions, both individually and collectively.


One part of my work looks at individual choices: for example, what motivates effort in contests, how people invest when they face algorithmic discrimination, why they avoid ambiguity, and whether knowing their relative income rank changes how much they give to charity.


I’m also interested in cooperation and collective decision-making. At Galway, I work on the LE CHÉILE project, studying how communities can work together on climate action and experimentally testing what helps foster and sustain cooperation.


I hold an MSc in Applied Economics from UCD and a BBA in International Business from National Chengchi University.


Research Interests: Behavioral Economics, Experimental Economics, Game Theory, Environmental Economics, Public Economics
